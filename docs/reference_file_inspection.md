## Data quality and validation

Observed issues include vehicle-type capitalization differences (`10mt`/`10MT`), potential conflicting master attributes across months, duplicate vehicle suffixes, blank invoice/transporter fields, inconsistent transporter values, and Excel numeric formatting of identifiers. Exact conflict counts are written to the local validation workbooks generated by the master builder. Conflicting vehicle attributes are left blank in the master instead of being silently selected.
